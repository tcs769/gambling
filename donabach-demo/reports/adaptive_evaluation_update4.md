# Four-way evaluation and the value of judgment over time (sample household)

Generated by `python -m app.study.adaptive_evaluation`. Nothing was tuned toward a result.

* Household: the demo household (two earners aged 38 and 37; $150k brokerage, $400k pre-tax, $30k Roth, $40k cash; a home purchase in 3 years, college in 10, retirement at 62). Horizon: age 80 (42 years).
* Best fixed strategy (what the search chooses from the 1,008-strategy equity-first menu): **Hold the next 6 years of what you will need to take out of the portfolio in bonds and private credit and everything else in stocks, paying expenses and taxes from bonds and private credit first and not selling stocks to refill the reserve after a year they fall; fill the 24% bracket with Roth conversions every year; keep a 15% private credit sleeve (it counts toward the reserve), in the pre-tax account first, then the Roth, then the taxable account; harvest losses stock by stock with direct indexing; when stocks are sold in the taxable account, sell the shares that cost the most first.**
* Evaluation: 1,000 simulated futures the search never saw; every strategy is run on the same futures. Risk aversion 3; wealth below $50,000 counts as $50,000 in the certainty equivalent.
* The adaptive planner runs at the defaults: 32 rollouts per candidate, two coordinate-search passes, each proposed change re-tested on 64 fresh futures. It took 15.3 seconds per 100 paths on this machine's 4 cores.
* Advantages are annualized growth-rate differences (see `app/study/metrics.py`); ranges are the 5th to 95th percentile over resamples of the futures.

## What judgment over time adds

The adaptive planner against the best fixed strategy, on the same futures: median +0.01%/yr (-0.00% to +0.03%) ($34,753 at 80); risk-adjusted +0.01%/yr (+0.00% to +0.02%) ($1,612 at 80).
