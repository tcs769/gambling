# Is the plan holding too little equity?

Generated by `python -m app.study.equity_share`. Nothing was tuned toward a result, and nothing the app does was changed.

* Household: the demo household (two earners aged 38 and 37; a home purchase in 3 years, college in 10, retirement at 62). Horizon: age 80.
* Plans are chosen on 6,000 futures and scored on 2,000 other futures they never saw; the good advisor is on the same futures as every plan.
* The app's recommendation (risk aversion 3, 400 futures, seed 2026): **Hold 50% of the money outside the private credit sleeve in stocks (42.5% of the portfolio) until 58, then glide down; fill the 24% bracket with Roth conversions every year; keep a 15% private credit sleeve, in the pre-tax account first, then the Roth, then the taxable account; harvest losses at fund level.**
* *Raw advantage*: the growth-rate difference between median after-tax wealth at 80 and the advisor's, annualized over the 42 years. *Risk-adjusted advantage*: the same on certainty-equivalent wealth at risk aversion 3 with a $50,000 floor. 'Stocks' is the share of the whole portfolio, so a 50% stock share with a 15% private credit sleeve holds 42.5% in stocks.
* The advisor: 80% stocks today falling to 50% by 70, no private credit; median wealth at 80 $2,909,021, 10th percentile $941,390, 0.6% of futures run out of money.
