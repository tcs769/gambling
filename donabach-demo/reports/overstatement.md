# Overstatement checks

The plan chosen under the specified assumptions, held fixed, on 2,000 fresh futures; one assumption changed at a time. Median and risk-adjusted advantage over the good RIA, a year.

## The showcase household (Tanaka)

Plan: 6-year spending reserve, the rest in stocks, fill 24% bracket (every year), 15% private credit, direct indexing.

| Assumption | Median advantage | Risk-adjusted | Ruin chance |
|---|---|---|---|
| as specified | 2.10% | 0.58% | 2.9% |
| direct indexing costs 0.10% a year in trading | 2.05% (-0.05 points) | 0.55% | 2.9% |
| direct indexing costs 0.15% a year in trading | 2.03% (-0.08 points) | 0.53% | 2.9% |
| wash-sale rule disallows 24% of harvested losses | 2.08% (-0.02 points) | 0.54% | 2.9% |
| private credit returns 2.0% (not 3.5%) | 1.80% (-0.31 points) | 0.28% | 3.4% |
| private credit returns 5.0% | 2.40% (+0.30 points) | 0.86% | 2.2% |
| private credit volatility 12% (not 8%) | 2.03% (-0.07 points) | 0.35% | 3.2% |
| stocks' own movement 15% a year (not 25%) | 2.07% (-0.03 points) | 0.57% | 2.9% |
| stocks' own movement 35% a year | 2.12% (+0.02 points) | 0.58% | 2.9% |
| trading cost 0.10%, wash sale 24% and private credit 2.0% together | 1.72% (-0.38 points) | 0.20% | 3.4% |
