# How often each stock share wins (100 synthetic households)

100 synthetic households (the training-data generator, seed 1), each searched on 150 simulated futures.
Before: the menu offered 70/80/90/100% stocks. After (spec A2): 50/60/70/80/90/100%. Same households, same futures.

| Stock share | Wins before | Wins after |
|---|---|---|
| 50% | not offered | 23 |
| 60% | not offered | 17 |
| 70% | 51 | 11 |
| 80% | 6 | 6 |
| 90% | 6 | 6 |
| 100% | 37 | 37 |

40 of 100 households changed their winning share when the menu widened.

| Was | Became | Households |
|---|---|---|
| 70% | 50% | 23 |
| 70% | 60% | 17 |
