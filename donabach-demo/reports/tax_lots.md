# What tax lots change

Generated by `python -m app.study.tax_lots`. The sample household (the spec's fictional household), good RIA baseline. Each row searches the planning style's strategy menu on 400 futures and scores the winner on 2,000 futures it never saw; the last two columns average 5 seeds. Nothing is tuned: the rows differ only in the lot rules.

* **Lot tracking.** The brokerage stock is the opening position (long-term) plus one lot per year, which takes that year's reinvested dividends, rebalancing purchases and savings at cost. Loss harvesting is still the aggregate rule: it lowers every lot's basis in proportion.
* **Sale order.** Donabach sells the lot bought at the highest price first (the least gain per dollar); the advisors sell the oldest first.
* **Short-term gains.** A lot sold in the year it was bought has a short-term gain, one sold the next year a half-short-term gain (purchases and sales are spread through a year, so it has been held more than a year half the time), an older one a long-term gain. The short-term part is ordinary income, net of short-term and long-term losses.
