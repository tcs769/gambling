# Update 4: single-household measurements

## The sample household (Alex and Jordan Sample)

**The plan the planner recommends:** 6-year spending reserve, the rest in stocks, fill 24% bracket (every year), 15% private credit, direct indexing (direct indexing on).

Annualized advantage in wealth at 80, median / risk-adjusted, on 2,000 futures the plan was not searched on:

| Against | Liquidated at 80 | Passed to heirs at 80 |
|---|---|---|
| Good advisor | +1.80% / +0.06% | +1.87% / +0.17% |
| Typical advisor (its funds pay capital-gain distributions) | +2.55% / +1.18% | +2.65% / +1.31% |
| Typical advisor before distributions were added | +2.49% / +1.10% | +2.59% / +1.22% |

Choosing the plan for the heirs' valuation instead gives: 6-year spending reserve, the rest in stocks, fill 24% bracket (every year), 15% private credit, direct indexing; +1.80% liquidated and +1.87% passed to heirs over the good advisor.
